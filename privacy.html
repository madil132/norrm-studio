Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. How Norrm Studio protects your privacy and business data.

1. Overview

At Norrm Studio (“we”, “our”, or “us”), based in Lahore, Pakistan, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal and business information you share with us through our website (norrmstudio.com), discovery consultations, email correspondence, or client services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and business/clinic name when you fill out our contact form or book a call via Calendly.
  • Business context, market goals, and practice requirements shared during discovery calls or strategy inquiries.
  • Standard website analytics such as browser type, device information, and pages visited to help us optimize site performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for legitimate agency operations:

We treat all business metrics, ad spend figures, lead data, and patient acquisition pipelines with strict confidentiality. Any case studies or public testimonials are published only with prior written consent from our clients.

Our site utilizes trusted third-party services such as Calendly for calendar scheduling. When you interact with third-party widgets, their respective privacy policies govern the data transmitted to their platforms.
